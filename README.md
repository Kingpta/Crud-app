**NetGuard NG**  
**A Simple Solution to Nigeria’s Communications Gaps**

-----

### What is NetGuard NG?

NetGuard NG is a free mobile app and website that lets everyday Nigerians check and report how good or bad their phone network and internet really are — and helps them get fair treatment when the service is poor.

-----

### What It Does (in Simple Terms)

- **Shows the real network quality around you**  
  People test their signal strength, internet speed, and call quality, then share the results. The app creates a live map of Nigeria showing which areas and which networks (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile) are working well or badly at any moment.
- **Tracks if your data is disappearing too fast**  
  It helps you see whether the data you paid for is being used normally or vanishing strangely, so you have clear proof if something feels wrong.
- **Helps you claim compensation easily**  
  When the network is very bad or stays down for a long time, the app helps you create a proper complaint with evidence. You can send it to the network company and escalate it to the Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC) if needed. This follows the rules NCC already made that operators must compensate customers for poor service.
- **Highlights places with no network**  
  It is especially useful for villages and rural areas that still have little or no coverage. It clearly shows the regulator exactly where the biggest gaps are.
- **Gives the NCC better information**  
  The Commission receives clear, real-time data from thousands of ordinary users instead of relying only on what the big telecom companies report.

-----

### Short Summary

NetGuard NG turns the daily frustration of bad networks, fast-finishing data, and poor service into a simple tool that:

1. Shows the truth about network quality across Nigeria
2. Helps ordinary people prove problems and claim compensation
3. Gives the NCC solid evidence to force better service and close the remaining communications gaps

It is practical, can be built quickly by a small team, and directly addresses the biggest complaints Nigerians are making online and on social media right now.
